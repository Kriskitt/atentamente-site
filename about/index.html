Belén Valverde + Qenqo

Creative Duo

Each project pursued by the studio, be that a book, an identity, film, exhibition or installation, grows out of a process of conceptually rigorous investigation, with Knight and McGrath attending closely to both the formal properties and the contextual nexus within which the project participates. A project is thus a proposition, a means to interrogate beliefs, procedures, behavioural, temporal and material realities, and (re)consider what it means to be in the world, and work together.
